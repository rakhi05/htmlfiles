Steps to Bake a Cake

  1. Gather all ingredients like flour, sugar, eggs, and butter.
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  3. Mix the dry ingredients in one bowl and wet ingredients in another.
  4. Combine both mixtures and stir until smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased baking tray.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes or until golden brown.
  7. Let the cake cool, then decorate with cream or frosting.
